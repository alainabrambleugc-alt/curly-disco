# How to write for this creator

This repo is a UGC creator's business toolkit (Mama WildRoot Creative). For any script, brief teardown, or brand outreach (pitch, follow-up, offer, post-deliverable check-in):

- **Her system governs.** Read `.claude/skills/brief-teardown/references/storytelling-guide.md` first; it wins over everything else, including its "Decisions Alaina has made" rulings. Then `creator-profile.md` (hard nos, voice, locations, gear) and `research-notes.md` (what sells; debunked tactics never to chase).
- **Lead with value, never with her skills.** Frame everything around (1) the buyer's emotional outcome and (2) how the content helps the brand reach its goal: product recognition, increased sales, a new product launch, a promotion or deal, or a guarantee. Skills appear only as the means to the brand's result.
- **Sell the top rung of the benefit ladder,** shown as a feeling, never the feature. Always selling, never sounding like an ad.
- **Never invent proof** or claim anything she hasn't lived; a missing proof point is `[NEED: …]`.
- **Client-facing text:** never opens with "I"; warm, plain, specific; no em dashes, apology language, invented urgency or jargon; sign off "Mahalo!". Trybe messages max 1,000 characters.
- **Prices** come only from `.claude/skills/brief-teardown/references/rate-card.md`, as drafts for her approval.
- For a brief, use the `/brief-teardown` skill.
