# What the research changed (Nov 15, 2026 deep dive — the short version)

Alaina's research summary, saved 2026-11-16. Full report with sources and evidence grades is in Rumi's research notes; ask her for it any time. These findings are baked into the prompts (especially the views pass, Prompt 7). Use them to inform choices — never quote these numbers on camera or as Alaina's proof; they are research, not her results.

## Instagram's top signals (Mosseri)
Watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach — sends weigh most for reaching non-followers. Design for "send this to a friend who…" on organic posts.

## Hook window
Proposition by second 3, hook complete by second 6 (TikTok attributes most ad recall to the first 6 seconds).

## The sales-angle surprise (MediaLabs, 9,285 TikTok Shop videos watched, Sep–Oct 2026)
The angles that sold per view were urgency/scarcity 1.35×, value/deal 1.26×, social proof 1.21× — and curiosity was the WEAKEST at 0.71×. Problem→solution is the most common structure but only average as an angle (0.98×): it's the structure, not the pitch. Curiosity earns watches; proof and deal earn sales.

## Formats per view
Voiceover B-roll 1.24×, unboxing 1.20×, review/testimonial 1.17×, talking head 1.10× — and demo/tutorial, the most-made format, was below average per view (0.96×). The demo works as a shot inside the video, not as the whole format.

## Frame 1
Face + product + text together in the first second measured 1.07× sales per view vs 0.79× for none of them. Text alone did nothing.

## Creator-style creative converts
The one peer-reviewed field test (67 Meta campaigns) found creator-style ads lost on click-through but massively won on conversion and ROAS vs brand creative — judge UGC on sales, not clicks.

## Two ledgers
Across 129.6M engagements, engagement rate explained virtually none of brand memory. Distribution and capture get scored separately (Prompt 7, item 8).

## No magic length
Completion favors short; total watch time favors longer-if-held. Buffer's 1.1M TikToks: over 60s earned more reach and watch time than 30–60s. Length follows the arc.

## Debunked — the engine must NOT chase these
- "UGC gets 4× CTR" (untraceable)
- Karaoke captions as a retention device (no controlled evidence)
- Pattern interrupts every 2–3 seconds (no study)
- "85% watch muted" (2019 self-report)
- Hashtags / #fyp for reach (denied on record)
- Magic posting times
- "Demos convert 3–5× talking heads" (contradicted)
- Invented retention thresholds (no platform publishes one)

## Legal floor
Never generate a fake testimonial or fake personal experience; only true scarcity. (FTC — this matches Alaina's standing integrity rule.) A brand-approved dramatization with a legible on-screen "Actor portrayal" disclosure is not presented as a genuine testimonial; see storytelling guide ruling 13.

# Views Pass — Rule Grades (the missing notes for Prompt 7)

Evidence grades from the Nov 15, 2026 deep-dive research report ("UGC Views and Sales Research," full report with sources held by Rumi — ask any time). Saved 2026-11-16.

**Grade key:** Measured = large dataset, platform split test, peer-reviewed or field experiment · Platform-stated = the platform published it, method limited/unpublished · Operator opinion = practitioner/vendor practice, no controlled test. **SOFT rules are defaults, never hard fails.**

## Grades for Prompt 7's eight checks, in order

1. **Frame 1: face + product + text in the first second; product cue inside 2 seconds — MEASURED. STRONG.** Face + product + text together measured 1.07× sales/view vs 0.79× for none (MediaLabs, 9,285 TikTok Shop videos). Brand/product cue in the first 2 seconds: only 61% of creator ads had one, and early branding multiplied awareness lift (System1/Kantar).
2. **Proposition by second 3, hook complete by second 6; no logo/greeting/title card first — PLATFORM-STATED. STRONG as a platform rule**; the exact seconds are the platforms' framing, not a lab measurement.
3. **New information beat every 7–10s; visual change on every new thought; no static frame over 6s — OPERATOR OPINION. SOFT.** No controlled study establishes any interval. The principle (changes must carry information, not noise cuts) is the rule; the numbers are practitioner defaults.
4. **Phrase-level captions (3–7 words), full meaning muted; no karaoke highlighting as a retention device — MEASURED-NEGATIVE for karaoke.** No controlled evidence karaoke beats phrase captions; claims trace to caption-tool vendors; accessibility research favors phrase chunking. Captions themselves: survey/prevalence support. STRONG on "don't rely on karaoke," moderate on captions generally.
5. **Safe zones (top 14% / bottom 35% / sides 6%); 9:16, clean export, no watermarks — MEASURED / PLATFORM. STRONG** as a format-compliance rule (Meta Reels ads guidance; Meta's 15 split tests back native vertical video).
6. **End loops into frame 1, or closes on the payoff; no outro/end card — OPERATOR OPINION. SOFT.** Widely practiced, no controlled test found.
7. **Length serves the arc; every second past ~35 must hold proof — MIXED.** TikTok's ad band is ~21–34s; Buffer's 1.1M TikToks found longer videos earned more reach/watch time. There is NO magic length — **"no padding" is firm; any specific second count is SOFT.**
8. **Two-ledger scoring (distribution vs capture, never merged) — MEASURED. STRONG** — one of the best-supported rules in the set. Across 129.6M engagements, engagement rate explained virtually none of brand memory (System1/WPP/TikTok); Kantar found engagement and effectiveness aligned in only 1 in 3 creator assets.

## The wider rule set, graded (28 rules from the same report)

- Hook as a specific open question or number + consequence, never a vague tease — Measured (headline RCTs); video transfer is operator-level.
- One claim per video; word budget ~150–165 wpm less ~10% (~68–74 words at 30s) — Operator opinion/standard.
- Payoff delivered as early as honestly possible; never withhold the title's promise to the end — Operator opinion, consistent with platform satisfaction statements.
- ≤5 hashtags as labels only; keywords in speech, on-screen text, caption's first line — Platform-stated.
- Original footage only; never re-upload watermarked cross-posts — Platform-stated (Instagram originality rule).
- Sales structure hook → visible proof → specific result → one CTA; demo is a shot, not the whole format; VO B-roll pattern 1.24× — Measured associations + operator checklist.
- Angle led by value/deal (1.26×), social proof (1.21×), or true urgency (1.35×) — NOT pure curiosity (0.71×, weakest) and not problem/solution alone (0.98×) — Measured (MediaLabs).
- Specificity: every vague claim replaced by a specific, in-frame-verifiable number/timeframe/artefact — Measured (specificity lab research).
- Social proof as an artefact: real buyer-matched face + specific result, and/or sourced count + rating on screen — Measured (A/B + field); the in-video form is operator-level.
- One objection, conceded AND refuted in the same breath with visible proof — Measured (two-sided-argument meta-analysis).
- Lo-fi/native look by default, but proof clearly visible; inauthentic native loses (−19% purchase intent) — Platform-stated/measured.
- One CTA, after proof, specific and awareness-matched; CTA wording alone is not a proven lever — Platform-stated; wording is operator-level.
- Only true scarcity/loss framing; reject invented stock, countdowns, deadlines — Measured (urgency angle) + FTC dark-pattern guidance. Also a hard integrity rule for Alaina regardless of grade.
- Never generate a fake testimonial — FTC/platform-stated. LEGAL rule, not a performance tip.
- Identity named in the hook; creator framed as a member of the group — Operator opinion built on measured identity theory.
- Reciprocity: one complete, usable piece of value before any CTA — Measured in lab/field, unmeasured in video. TEST, don't assume.
- Authority shown by demonstration, never title alone — Measured at survey level + platform policy.
- Testing loop: hook variants first; judge hook rate → hold → click → conversion — Measured (vendor data).
- Cadence 2–5 posts/week beats timing optimization; use her own audience-active hours — Measured (Buffer, 11.4M posts).

## Debunked — the engine must NOT chase these (all checked and failed)
"UGC gets 4× CTR" (untraceable; the one peer-reviewed field test found creator creative LOWER on CTR, far higher on conversion) · karaoke captions as retention (no controlled evidence) · pattern interrupts every 2–3s (no study) · "85% watch muted" (2019 self-report) · hashtags/#fyp for reach (denied on record by both platforms) · magic posting times (largest studies disagree) · "demos convert 3–5× talking heads" (contradicted — demo format was BELOW average per view, 0.96×) · invented retention/hook-rate thresholds (no platform publishes one) · "a send is worth 3–5× a like" (no published source) · Zeigarnik-effect open-loop claims (2025 meta-analysis found no reliable memory advantage).

## Honest caveats the engine should know
- MediaLabs TikTok Shop data (the 1.35×/1.24×/0.71× figures) is observational, US-only, one 30-day window (Sep–Oct 2026), AI-tagged; sales-per-view is not a conversion rate. Strongest sales dataset found, but not causal.
- No controlled study of cliffhangers/open loops, pattern interrupts, or reciprocity IN short-form video was found — those rest on lab psychology + practice. A/B them on Alaina's own data.
- Headline platform lift numbers (Meta partnership 19%, etc.) are platforms' own lift-study averages via secondary summaries — direction corroborated, exact magnitudes not for external quoting.
